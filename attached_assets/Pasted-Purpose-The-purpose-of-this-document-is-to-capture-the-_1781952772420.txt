Purpose
The purpose of this document is to capture the business requirements for strengthening Procurement and Rate Contract (RC) Management in a generic inventory/procurement management system.
This requirement document specifically addresses the following challenges:

Monitoring of Rate Contracts (RCs) for essential medicines / products in the item master
Advance visibility and alerts for expiring RCs
Digitized tender lifecycle tracking and visibility on tender progress/sub-stages


3. Business Context
Organizations managing procurement of medicines, healthcare products, or other essential items often face significant challenges in maintaining visibility over Rate Contracts and tender activities.
Typical issues include:

Active rate contracts may be available only for a subset of the required item list
The current system may not clearly show:

which products have active RCs
which products do not have RCs
which RCs are nearing expiry
which products are covered through tender activity


Users may currently depend on:

reviewing external systems / portals
checking physical or scanned RC documents
following up manually with procurement teams


Advance alerting for expiring RCs may be absent
Tender processing may take several months and multiple tenders may run in parallel
There may be no clear visibility into tender stages, sub-stages, ownership, delays, or ageing
Tender records may not be fully digitized for tracking and monitoring

These issues lead to delayed renewals, insufficient procurement visibility, weak planning, and reduced control over the procurement lifecycle.

4. Objective
The solution shall provide a centralized mechanism to:

monitor RC availability across all relevant essential items / products
track RC validity and expiry dates
generate alerts at least 6 months before RC expiry
provide dashboard and exception views for items without active RCs
digitize tender records and monitor tender stage/sub-stage movement
improve management visibility over procurement progress, delays, and risks


5. Scope
5.1 In Scope
This requirement document includes:

item-wise RC tracking
RC availability monitoring
RC validity and expiry management
automated RC expiry alerts
tender master creation and maintenance
tender stage and sub-stage tracking
tender ageing and delay monitoring
reports and dashboards for review and decision-making

5.2 Out of Scope
The following are not part of this requirement document:

vendor payment processing
full e-procurement publishing platform functionality
bid evaluation automation
contract negotiation workflows
legal drafting automation
modification of external procurement or rate contract portals

7. Business Requirements

7.1 Requirement Area 1: Monitoring of Rate Contracts (RCs)
Business Need
A centralized view is required to monitor rate contracts for all essential medicines / products in the item master, including clear distinction between items with active RCs and those without active RCs.
Requirement Statements
BR-01
The system shall maintain and display the complete list of essential items / products for procurement tracking.
BR-02
The system shall allow mapping of rate contracts to individual items or product variants.
BR-03
The system shall clearly identify and display the procurement status of each item as one of the following:

Active RC Available
RC Not Available
RC Expiring Soon
RC Expired
Tender in Progress

BR-04
The system shall provide a dashboard showing:

total tracked items
count of items with active RC
count of items without RC
count of items with RC nearing expiry
count of items with expired RC
count of items where tender is in progress

BR-05
Users shall be able to search and filter RC status by:

item name
item code
RC status
expiry date
vendor / supplier
tender reference

BR-06
Users shall be able to view RC details for a selected item, including:

RC number
issue date
effective date
expiry date
vendor details
covered item details
uploaded RC document


7.2 Requirement Area 2: Expiring RCs and Lack of Advance Alerts
Business Need
RC expiry dates are often checked manually through external systems or stored documents. The system must proactively identify expiring contracts and provide alerts at least 6 months in advance to support timely renewal or tendering action.
Requirement Statements
BR-07
The system shall maintain RC issue date, effective date, and expiry date for each rate contract.
BR-08
The system shall automatically monitor RC expiry dates on a scheduled basis.
BR-09
The system shall generate alerts for RCs approaching expiry at least 180 days (6 months) before the expiry date.
BR-10
The system shall support additional configurable alert intervals such as:

120 days before expiry
90 days before expiry
60 days before expiry
30 days before expiry
expired status alert

BR-11
The system shall display expiring RCs in dashboard and worklist views for user action.
BR-12
Alerts shall include:

item name
item code
RC number
expiry date
number of days remaining
linked vendor
current tender / renewal status, if any

BR-13
The system shall highlight RCs where no tender or renewal action has been initiated despite nearing expiry.
BR-14
The system shall support escalation or priority highlighting for RCs that are close to expiry and still have no procurement action initiated.

7.3 Requirement Area 3: Long Procurement Cycle and Limited Visibility on Tender Progress
Business Need
Tender processing can take several months, and multiple tenders may run in parallel. There is a need for digitized tender records, sub-stage visibility, and ageing/delay monitoring.
Requirement Statements
BR-15
The system shall allow digitized creation and maintenance of tender master records.
BR-16
Each tender record shall be linkable to one or more items/products.
BR-17
The system shall enable tracking of each tender through predefined stages and sub-stages.
BR-18
The system shall provide visibility into the current stage and sub-stage of each tender.
BR-19
The system shall support simultaneous monitoring of multiple active tenders.
BR-20
The system shall capture and display ageing for each tender, including:

date of initiation
current stage start date
days spent in current stage
total tender duration

BR-21
The system shall identify delayed tenders or stages that exceed expected timelines.
BR-22
The system shall allow uploading and maintenance of digitized tender documents, such as:

tender file
approvals
bidder clarification documents
corrigendum documents
evaluation documents
award / contract documents

BR-23
The system shall provide a dashboard/worklist showing all ongoing tenders with:

tender reference number
linked items
current stage
current sub-stage
pending with
ageing
expected next action


8. Functional Requirements

8.1 Item Master & RC Management
FR-01
The system shall maintain an item master for all essential products / medicines / materials under procurement management.
FR-02
The system shall allow creation, update, and maintenance of Rate Contract master records.
FR-03
Each RC record shall include at minimum:

RC number
RC issue date
effective date
expiry date
vendor / supplier
item(s) covered
document attachment
remarks
active/inactive status

FR-04
The system shall map one or more RCs to items in the item master.
FR-05
The system shall derive item-wise RC status using business logic:

valid RC exists → Active RC Available
no RC exists → RC Not Available
RC exists and expiry threshold reached → RC Expiring Soon
RC expiry date passed → RC Expired
no active RC but tender ongoing → Tender in Progress


8.2 RC Expiry Monitoring and Alerts
FR-06
A scheduled job/process shall run daily to check RC expiry dates.
FR-07
The system shall generate automated alerts for expiring contracts at configured intervals beginning at 180 days before expiry.
FR-08
The system shall show a dedicated worklist for:

expiring RCs
expired RCs
RCs without renewal action
uncovered items

FR-09
The system shall support role-based access to alerts and worklists.
FR-10
The system shall enable visual prioritization of critical RCs nearing expiry.

8.3 Tender Management and Monitoring
FR-11
The system shall allow creation of tender master records with:

tender ID / reference
tender description
initiation date
linked item(s)
tender owner
current stage
current sub-stage
status
remarks

FR-12
The system shall maintain tender stage and sub-stage history.
FR-13
The system shall support stage/sub-stage configuration by admin.
FR-14
Suggested tender stages shall include:

Tender Planning
Document Preparation
Approval for Tender
Publication
Bid Receipt
Bid Query Handling
Technical Evaluation
Commercial Evaluation
Approval / Recommendation
Award / LOI
Contract Finalization
RC Creation / Closure

FR-15
The system shall capture stage movement date and user details for audit and monitoring.
FR-16
The system shall calculate:

stage-wise ageing
total tender ageing
overdue stage duration

FR-17
The system shall allow upload and storage of tender-related documents.
FR-18
The system shall allow linking of expiring/uncovered items with:

a new tender
an existing tender in progress

12. Business Rules
Rule-01
Every tracked item shall have a visible procurement coverage status.
Rule-02
If a valid RC exists for the current date range, the item shall be classified as Active RC Available.
Rule-03
If no valid RC exists and no tender exists, the item shall be classified as RC Not Available.
Rule-04
If no active RC exists but a tender is in progress, the item shall be classified as Tender in Progress.
Rule-05
The minimum default alert period for expiring RCs shall be 180 days.
Rule-06
Expired RCs shall be treated as critical and prominently highlighted.
Rule-07
Tender ageing shall be automatically calculated based on initiation date and stage transition dates.
Rule-08
All RC updates, tender updates, and stage changes shall be audit logged.
Rule-09
If multiple RCs are linked to the same item, the system shall determine the effective active RC based on validity dates and status.
