2. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to capture the business requirements for strengthening inventory visibility, warehouse control, supply chain monitoring, demand forecasting, analytics, and KPI-based decision support in a generic healthcare / inventory management environment.
This requirement document specifically addresses the following challenges:
1.	Stock lying unused at facilities due to procurement indents being raised without checking available stock
2.	Absence of a structured stock redistribution mechanism across facilities
3.	Extended quarantine and quality check process at warehouses with weak tracking and prioritization
4.	Lack of end-to-end stock visibility across warehouses, stores, and dispensing points
5.	Weak near-expiry monitoring and weak FEFO/FIFO adherence
6.	Weak or absent demand forecasting
7.	Weak district-level / regional distribution vs consumption analytics
8.	Weak tracking of standardized performance metrics and KPIs
________________________________________
3. Business Context
Organizations managing essential medicines and healthcare products frequently face operational inefficiencies due to fragmented inventory visibility, weak warehouse controls, manual tracking, and insufficient analytics.
Typical business problems include:
•	Facilities raising procurement indents without checking existing stock balances, resulting in excess or irrational procurement
•	Surplus stock lying unused in some facilities while other facilities face stock shortages
•	Quarantined stock remaining blocked for extended periods because sample collection, testing, and release are not actively monitored
•	Inadequate visibility of stock movement from warehouse to store to dispensing location
•	Poor monitoring of near-expiry batches, leading to wastage and weak FEFO/FIFO adherence
•	Limited use of consumption history and seasonal patterns for demand forecasting
•	Inability to compare procured, distributed, consumed, expired, and wasted quantities across geographies
•	No standardized operational KPIs for inventory, warehouse, and supply chain performance
These challenges result in stock imbalance, delayed availability, avoidable expiry, operational inefficiency, and weak decision-making.
________________________________________
4. Objective
The solution shall provide a centralized mechanism to:
•	validate procurement indents against available facility stock
•	identify and enable redistribution of surplus stock between facilities
•	track warehouse quarantine and quality check status
•	provide end-to-end stock visibility across the supply chain
•	monitor near-expiry stock and improve FEFO/FIFO adherence
•	support demand forecasting using historical consumption and seasonality
•	provide distribution vs consumption analytics across regions/facilities
•	define and monitor operational, inventory, and supply chain KPIs

5. Scope
5.1 In Scope
This requirement document includes:
•	facility stock visibility during indent creation
•	indent validation based on available stock and usage
•	inter-facility stock redistribution
•	warehouse quarantine and quality check status tracking
•	release/rejection monitoring of quarantined stock
•	end-to-end stock movement tracking

•	district / regional distribution vs consumption analytics

5.2 Out of Scope
The following are not part of this requirement document:
•	vendor payment processing
•	financial accounting
•	transportation route optimization

7. Business Requirements
________________________________________
7.1 Requirement Area 1: Stock Visibility at Facilities and Rational Indenting
Business Need
Facilities must not raise procurement indents without visibility into available stock, stock in transit, blocked stock, and usage trends.
Requirement Statements
BR-01
The system shall display current stock position at facility level before an indent is raised.
BR-02
The system shall show, at minimum, the following item-wise stock information during indent creation:
•	stock on hand
•	usable stock
•	blocked / non-usable stock
•	stock in transit
•	pending approved indent quantity
•	near-expiry stock
•	average consumption
•	stock cover
BR-03
The system shall validate whether the requested indent quantity is justified based on available stock and consumption trends.
BR-04
The system shall identify indents that indicate overstock risk or irrational demand.
BR-05
The system shall allow warnings and/or hard-stop controls for indents that exceed configured stock norms.
BR-06
The system shall require remarks/justification when requested quantities exceed system recommendations.
________________________________________
7.2 Requirement Area 2: Stock Redistribution Between Facilities
Business Need
A mechanism is required to identify excess stock at some facilities and shortages at others and allow structured stock transfer.
Requirement Statements
BR-07
The system shall identify facilities with surplus stock based on configurable thresholds.
BR-08
The system shall identify facilities with shortage or stock-out risk.
BR-09
The system shall provide a recommendation mechanism to match surplus stock with shortage locations.
BR-10
The system shall support creation, approval, dispatch, receipt, and closure of inter-facility stock transfers.
BR-11
The system shall consider batch, expiry, quantity, and location while recommending redistribution.
BR-12
Near-expiry stock shall be prioritized for redistribution to high-consumption locations where feasible.
________________________________________
7.3 Requirement Area 3: Quarantine and Quality Check Tracking at Warehouse
Business Need
Quarantined medicines must be actively tracked to avoid prolonged delays in sample collection, testing, and release.
Requirement Statements
BR-13
The system shall maintain quarantine status for stock received at warehouse.
BR-14
The system shall track stage-wise statuses for quarantined stock, including:
•	received
•	under quarantine
•	sample collection pending
•	sample collected
•	sent for testing
•	result awaited
•	approved for release
•	rejected
•	released
BR-15
The system shall capture ageing for quarantined stock.
BR-16
The system shall prioritize lots nearing quarantine SLA breach for faster action.
BR-17
The system shall prevent quarantined stock from being treated as available stock until release.
BR-18
The system shall record release or rejection outcome along with supporting remarks/documents.
________________________________________
7.4 Requirement Area 4: End-to-End Stock Visibility
Business Need
Users require end-to-end visibility of stock movement from warehouse to stores and onward to dispensing/consumption points.
Requirement Statements
BR-19
The system shall provide stock visibility across all levels of the supply chain hierarchy.
BR-20
The system shall track item-wise and batch-wise movement through:
•	receipt
•	storage
•	issue
•	transfer
•	receipt at destination
•	consumption / dispensing
•	expiry / wastage
BR-21
The system shall support drill-down visibility from central warehouse / regional warehouse to facility store and dispensing locations.
BR-22
The system shall display stock status by location, including:
•	available stock
•	blocked stock
•	stock in transit
•	near-expiry stock
•	expired stock
BR-23
The system shall provide visibility of stock coverage and risk indicators such as stock-out risk or overstock.
________________________________________
7.5 Requirement Area 5: Near-Expiry Monitoring and FEFO/FIFO Adherence
Business Need
Near-expiry stock must be monitored proactively to minimize expiry and wastage and improve issue discipline.
Requirement Statements
BR-24
The system shall identify batches approaching expiry using configurable thresholds.
BR-25
The system shall generate alerts for near-expiry stock at warehouse and facility levels.
BR-26
The system shall support FEFO/FIFO-based recommendation at issue/dispatch stage.
BR-27
The system shall track FEFO/FIFO deviation where users issue non-priority batches.
BR-28
The system shall enable action tracking for near-expiry stock, such as:
•	priority issue
•	redistribution
•	block / hold
•	disposal planning
BR-29
The system shall provide visibility into likely expiry risk based on stock quantity, remaining shelf life, and consumption trend.
________________________________________
7.6 Requirement Area 6: Demand Forecasting
Business Need
Historical data should be used to forecast future demand considering consumption patterns and seasonality.
Requirement Statements
BR-30
The system shall support demand forecasting using historical issue / consumption data.
BR-31
The system shall allow forecasting by item, category, geography, and facility type.
BR-32
The system shall support configurable forecasting methods such as:
•	moving average
•	weighted moving average
•	seasonal average
•	trend-based estimate
BR-33
The system shall allow consideration of seasonal disease patterns or programmatic demand variations.
BR-34
The system shall allow exclusion or adjustment of abnormal periods such as stock-out months or emergency spikes.
BR-35
The system shall support manual review and override of forecasted quantities with reason capture.
________________________________________
7.7 Requirement Area 7: Distribution vs Consumption Analytics
Business Need
Decision-makers require visibility into the relationship between what is procured, distributed, consumed, expired, and wasted.
Requirement Statements
BR-36
The system shall calculate and display, by item and geography:
•	procured quantity
•	received quantity
•	distributed quantity
•	consumed / dispensed quantity
•	stock on hand
•	near-expiry quantity
•	expired quantity
•	wasted / damaged quantity
BR-37
The system shall support district / regional / facility-level comparison of distribution versus consumption.
BR-38
The system shall identify locations with:
•	repeated overstock
•	repeated stock-out
•	low consumption despite high distribution
•	high expiry / wastage
BR-39
The system shall support reporting and trend analysis for distributed vs consumed vs expired/wasted stock.
________________________________________
7.8 Requirement Area 8: KPI-Based Monitoring
Business Need
Operational, inventory, and supply chain processes require standardized KPIs for monitoring, exception handling, and decision-making.
Requirement Statements
BR-40
The system shall support KPI definition and monitoring across inventory, warehouse, and supply chain processes.
BR-41
The system shall display KPI dashboards for management and operational users.
BR-42
The system shall allow threshold-based categorization of KPIs (e.g., red/amber/green).
BR-43
The system shall support KPI alerts when thresholds are breached.
BR-44
The system shall support historical KPI trends over time.
BR-45
The system shall include meaningful KPIs such as:
•	stock-out rate
•	overstock rate
•	near-expiry stock %
•	expiry / wastage %
•	average quarantine time
•	on-time release %
•	redistribution turnaround time
•	forecast accuracy
•	issue-to-consumption lag
•	FEFO compliance %

12. Business Rules
Rule-01
Indent validation shall consider current stock, stock in transit, pending approved quantities, and historical usage.
Rule-02
Excess stock at one facility and shortage at another shall be considered for redistribution before fresh replenishment where applicable.
Rule-03
Only usable, non-expired, non-quarantine stock shall be eligible for transfer.
Rule-04
Near-expiry stock transfer shall be allowed only if minimum residual shelf life rules are met.
Rule-05
Quarantined stock shall not be considered available for issue or transfer.
Rule-06
FEFO shall be the preferred inventory issue principle; FIFO may apply where expiry data is not relevant or not available.
Rule-07
Forecast calculations shall support adjustment for stock-out periods and abnormal spikes.
Rule-08
KPI thresholds shall be configurable by administrators.
Rule-09
All stock movements, transfer actions, quarantine stage changes, and forecast overrides shall be audit logged.
